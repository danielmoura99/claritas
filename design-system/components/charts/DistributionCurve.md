# DistributionCurve

Bell curve of where price may close, with the probability of hitting a target — morphs live as the session moves.

```jsx
<DistributionCurve mean={38.6} sd={0.55} target={39} decimals={2} caption="chance de ≥ 39,00 no fechamento" />
```

- Dashed grey = prior (abertura); solid = now; sand dashed = mean.
